# Data Dictionary

## hotels
Real accommodation records used by the planner.

Important fields:
- `hotel_id`
- `name`
- `city`
- `price_from_xaf`
- `price_confidence`
- `beach_access`
- `pool`
- `wifi`
- `restaurant`
- `family_friendly`
- `source_url`
- `verified_on`

## transport
Public transport offers currently integrated for Douala/Yaoundé → Kribi.

## activities
Tourism activities and excursions.

## attractions
Tourist attractions and points of interest.

## restaurants
Restaurants and selected public menu prices.

## data_sources
Provenance registry used to keep the project auditable.
